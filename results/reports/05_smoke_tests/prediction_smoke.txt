FLAG: pre-FoG positive rate is ~1-4% of windows across every candidate and
the TEST subject, versus DETECTION's ~19% positive rate (Daphnet's native

window=64/step=32 predicts: each onset contributes at most ~2 positive
windows by construction. TAKE THIS AS A DIAGNOSTIC FINDING, not a bug -- it
is an intrinsic property of the task definition (S3), not a preprocessing
error.

CAVEATS on this comparison: (a) the PREDICTION run used n_epochs=10 (smoke
cap) vs DETECTION's 50 -- some of the F1/PR-AUC gap likely narrows with full
training, though the class-imbalance gap (1-4% vs ~19% positive) will not go
away with more epochs; (b) different val_subj was selected (S07 vs S06) since
inner scoring used PREDICTION-task labels; (c) window-level F1/PR-AUC are
known to be harsh under this level of imbalance -- see the episode-level
numbers in section 5, which tell a materially different (more encouraging)
story: 78.3% of freeze episodes DID get at least one correct imminent-onset
signal, with useful lead time (~0.70s of the 1.00s horizon).

7. BOTTOM LINE

- Pipeline is CORRECT: RF<=window holds, leakage guard holds, exclusion mask
  demonstrably removes freeze periods (13.99%/5.50% of samples), onset

  for this task and suggests the model IS picking up some pre-onset signal,
  just not reliably enough window-by-window to score well on F1/PR-AUC.

  this for the full 8-fold run -- some outer folds may have even sparser
  inner candidates than S01's fold did.
