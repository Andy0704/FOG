3. ESTIMATED WALL-CLOCK FOR THE FULL 40-TRIAL / 3-FOLD / 25-EPOCH STUDY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Projected from the smoke's observed per-epoch cost (14 models, 96.4s total,
avg 1.38s/epoch):

  40 trials x 3 outer folds x 7 inner candidates x 25 epochs
  = 28,917s (~482.0 min, ~8.03 hr) -- CONSERVATIVE UPPER BOUND

This upper bound assumes every trial runs every fold to completion at the
full 25-epoch cap with NO early stopping and NO pruning benefit. In
practice:
  - patience=5 early stopping will trigger before epoch 25 on many splits
    (the smoke's 5-epoch cap was too short for patience=5 to ever trigger
    -- most splits were still within their patience window at epoch 5 --
    so this upper bound does not yet reflect any early-stopping savings;
    expect real wall-clock to be MEANINGFULLY BELOW 8.03hr once the
    25-epoch cap gives patience=5 room to actually fire).
  - MedianPruner (n_startup_trials=8, n_warmup_steps=1) can abort a
    clearly-bad trial after just 1 of the 3 tuning folds, once >=8 trials
    have completed and given the pruner a real median to compare against
    -- this only helps from roughly trial 9 onward.
  - For reference, a lighter config (15 trials x 1 fold x 25 epochs) upper
    bound is ~1.00hr -- included by the harness as cost context, not a
    recommendation to switch (the stated 40/3/25 config was launched as
    specified).

Given "the full night" is available, ~8hr upper bound (likely less in
practice) fits comfortably within an overnight window.
