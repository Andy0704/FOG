NOTE ON THIS RUN: the first smoke attempt crashed on Trial 0 with a
PyTorch state_dict shape-mismatch RuntimeError. Root cause: run_split() (and
trainer_tcn.py) saved every training run's checkpoint to a single hardcoded
'best_model.pth' in the repo's CWD. A second, independent Claude Code session
was concurrently running its own training job (PREDICTION-task work, ADR-014)
against the SAME repo at the SAME time, and both processes were writing to
that same shared filename. Because this smoke varies model architecture
(kernel_size/dilations) between calls, the collision surfaced immediately as
a tensor-shape mismatch instead of silently corrupting a checkpoint. Fixed by
giving every run_split() call its own unique checkpoint path
(results/_tmp_checkpoints/ckpt_<pid>_<uuid>.pth, cleaned up by the caller
after use) in src/trainer_tcn.py + src/run_loso.py. This also protects any
future concurrent training runs from either session, not just Optuna's. The
smoke below is the RE-RUN, after the fix, which completed cleanly.
