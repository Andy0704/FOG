Read carefully -- the three metrics tell a MIXED story, not a uniform win:
- Test_ROC_AUC (rank-based, threshold-independent) improved substantially
  AND got MORE consistent across folds (std shrank by ~42%). This is the
  cleanest, most trustworthy improvement: the tuned model ranks positive
  vs negative windows better across every single fold, including
  previously-hard ones (S08 rose from 0.4908 to 0.7321; S07 rose from a
  presumably-lower baseline fold value to 0.7847).
- Test_PR_AUC (also rank-based but reweighted by class imbalance)
  improved even MORE in absolute terms (+0.1320, the largest gain of the
  three) but its std GREW (~46%). Some folds (S02=0.7683, S09=0.6851)
  improved dramatically; others (S06=0.3529, S01=0.4097) improved only
  modestly relative to the mean gain -- the improvement is real but
  uneven across subjects.
- Test_F1 (threshold-dependent -- frozen on val, applied to test, S2) is
  the WEAK LINK: mean improvement is small (+0.0201) and std grew
  sharply (~53%, from 0.144 to 0.220). Three folds have LOW F1 despite
  good-to-great ROC-AUC (S08 F1=0.091 despite ROC-AUC=0.732; S07 F1=0.183
  despite ROC-AUC=0.785; S06 F1=0.272 despite ROC-AUC=0.847) -- the
  Youden's-J-selected threshold from val is evidently not transferring to
  test as reliably under this hyperparameter combination as it did under
  the baseline's. This is worth investigating before treating F1 as a
  settled win (see section 5).

4. NOTABLE FINDING: S08 IS NO LONGER BELOW CHANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
S08 (the akinetic-phenotype outlier, diagnosed in docs/case_study_S08.md
and ADR-012 as a genuine class-conditional generalization failure under
the ORIGINAL baseline's hyperparameters, Test_ROC_AUC=0.4908) rises to
Test_ROC_AUC=0.7321 under trial26's hyperparameters -- a +0.2413 absolute
gain, by far the largest single-fold change in the entire comparison, and
enough to move it from "below chance" to comfortably above 0.70. This
was NOT a specific target of the Optuna tuning (S08 was one of 3 tuning
folds, ADR-018, but the objective was the MEAN across folds, not S08
specifically). Its F1 remains low (0.0909) -- the ranking quality
improved dramatically but the frozen threshold still does not suit this
subject well. This finding is significant enough to warrant its own
follow-up (updating docs/case_study_S08.md is a natural next step, not
done as part of this task since it wasn't requested here).
