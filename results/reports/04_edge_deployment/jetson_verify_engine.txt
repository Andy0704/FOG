----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CONCLUSION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Engines are numerically consistent with the source ONNX/PyTorch pipeline.

Both the FP32 engine (the confirmed deployment target per the Task 3 build
report, since FP16 showed no speedup on this model) and the FP16 engine
(built regardless, informational) reproduce onnxruntime's output within a
tight margin. The trt_fp32-vs-trt_fp16 gap (max 2.2e-04) is the same order
of magnitude as the ort-vs-fp16 gap (max 2.08e-04), as expected -- FP16's
error is dominated by its own reduced precision, not by any TensorRT-vs-ONNX
graph-execution discrepancy.
